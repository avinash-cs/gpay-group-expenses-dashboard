How to get your Google Pay Group Expenses Data

  1. Go to Google Takeout and sign in.
  2. Click Deselect all at the top of the list.
  3. Scroll down and check the box next to Google Pay.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and click Next step, then Create export.
  5. Download and extract the ZIP file once it is ready.
  6. Look for a file named Group expenses.json inside the folder.
  7. Upload that file using the button above to view your dashboard!
